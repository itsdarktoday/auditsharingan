# Third-party material

The release does not bundle upstream repository snapshots. The research notes
name the upstream projects used to inform the methodology, but those projects
are not runtime dependencies and are not required for skill discovery.

The evaluation corpus includes third-party testing material under
`evals/corpus/lib/forge-std`. Its license files stay with that material and
take precedence over the root `LICENSE` for those files.

The runtime code, prompts, schemas, templates, and knowledge files authored
for AuditSharingan are covered by the root project license unless a file says
otherwise.
